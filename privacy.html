Privacy Notice

How we collect, use and look after your personal information.

Zion Convergence is a prayer and discipleship community. We respect your privacy and only collect the information we need to serve you. This notice explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you fill in one of our forms, we collect the details you choose to give us:

  • Membership registration form: your name, contact details, location and the answers you give about joining the community.
  • School of Intimacy registration: your name, email, WhatsApp number, country and city, how you heard about us, and what you hope to gain.
  • Serve with us form: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, the areas you would like to serve in, and any message.
  • Next retreat notification: your name, email and, if you choose, your WhatsApp number.

This website does not use cookies or tracking tools of its own.

How we use it

  • To share meeting links, reminders and updates about our gatherings and programmes.
  • To welcome new members and keep in touch with the community.
  • To follow up when you offer to serve.
  • To tell you when the next retreat is announced.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your information is kept in the Zion Convergence Google account (Google Forms and Google Sheets) and is only seen by our leadership team, who need it to carry out the purposes above. We do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

Photos and videos

We sometimes take photos and videos at our gatherings and share them on our website, YouTube channel and WhatsApp community. Our registration form asks for your consent to this. If you would like a photo or video of you removed, email us and we will take it down.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you are part of the community or need the service you signed up for. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you. Just email zionconvergence@gmail.com.

Contact

For any question about this notice, email zionconvergence@gmail.com.
