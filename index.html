About us

Who we are

Zion Convergence is a prophetic prayer and discipleship community where God's people gather to seek His presence, grow in intimacy with Him and discover their true identity in Christ.

It is a place where believers are awakened to who they are and to the kingdom assignment God has given them.

We began as a prayer gathering for women and have grown into a community for men and women, new believers and mature believers alike.

Why we exist

Vision and mission

Vision

A community of believers who have been delivered, developed and deployed: living in God's presence, secure in their divine identity and boldly advancing His kingdom.

Mission

To lead God's people into intimacy with Him through His Word, prayer, deliverance and holiness, equipping them to walk in divine purpose and possess their spiritual inheritance.

Our model

Our model: the Acts 2 community

Acts 2:42

“And they continued steadfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, in the breaking of bread, and in prayers.”

At Pentecost God poured out His Spirit on sons and daughters alike. The believers who followed lived as a devoted, praying, generous community. That is the community we want to be.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can join?

Everyone. Our Friday prayer meetings, Bible discussions and annual retreat are for women. Crossover Prayers and the School of Intimacy are open to all.

Do I have to leave my church?

No. We are non-denominational, and we encourage every member to stay rooted in a local church.

Does it cost anything?

No. All our online gatherings are free.

How do I join a meeting?

Join our WhatsApp community. Meeting links are shared there, and the Google Meet links are also listed under Gatherings.

What time zone are the meetings in?

West Africa Time (WAT, UTC+1). Members join from different countries.

How do I become a member?

Fill in the registration form, read our Statement of Faith and Membership Covenant, and choose “Yes” to membership on the form.

I missed a session. Can I watch it?

School of Intimacy sessions are published on our YouTube channel.

How can I serve?

Fill in the Serve with us form and tell us whether you are interested in prayer, facilitation, media or event planning.
